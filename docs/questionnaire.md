# Business Questionnaire — i am ONE with

> This questionnaire exists so Claude can fully understand the business before writing copy, building pages, or making decisions. Answer as much as you can — even rough notes are useful. Save your answers in `docs/business-profile.md`.

---

## 1. About Kristie (the founder)

- What's your full name and how do you like to be referred to on the site?
- Where are you based? (city/suburb — for local SEO and event listings)
- What's your background story? How did you come to this work?
- What certifications or training do you hold? (e.g. Reiki Master, psychic training, etc.)
- How long have you been practising?
- Is this your full-time work or alongside something else?
- Do you want your personal story front and centre, or kept more subtle?
- Do you have a professional photo of yourself for the site?

---

## 2. Services — Reiki

- What level of Reiki do you offer? (e.g. Usui Reiki, Reiki Master)
- Session formats: in-person only, distance/remote, or both?
- Session lengths and prices? (e.g. 60 min / $120)
- Where are in-person sessions held? (home studio, rented space, client's home?)
- What does a session involve — what should a client expect?
- Any contraindications or who it's not suitable for?
- Do you offer packages (e.g. 3-session bundle)?

## 3. Services — Psychic Readings

- What type of readings do you offer? (e.g. intuitive, tarot, mediumship, akashic records?)
- Formats: in-person, phone, video call, email?
- Lengths and prices?
- What should a client expect from a reading?
- Any ethical boundaries you want stated clearly? (e.g. "I do not predict death or medical outcomes")
- Do you record readings for clients?
